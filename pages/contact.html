FAQ

Before You Ask

Quick answers to our most common inquiries regarding appointments and consultations.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

While we welcome walk-ins to browse our fabric library or view samples, all measurements, fittings, and dedicated style consultations require an advance appointment to ensure a master tailor is available for you.

Is there a consultation fee?

Initial bespoke consultations are complimentary. Should you choose to proceed, the cost is rolled into the deposit for your first commission. Dedicated personal styling services (without garment purchase) carry a separate fee structure.

How far in advance should I book for a wedding suit?

We strongly recommend booking your first consultation at least 4 to 5 months prior to your wedding date. This allows comfortable time for the 10-14 week construction process, plus any final micro-adjustments.

What should I bring to my first fitting?

For your measurements and subsequent fittings, please bring the style of dress shoes you plan to wear with the suit, and a well-fitting dress shirt. This ensures the trouser break and sleeve pitch are calibrated perfectly.
