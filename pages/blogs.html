In a world driven by speed and mass production, the bespoke suit stands as a testament to everything that endures. At Majestic Measure, each commission begins not with cloth and needle, but with a conversation.

The floating canvas, the hand-padded lapel, the surgically measured sleeve pitch these are not mere details. They are the language of a garment that moves with you, rather than against you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our readers about tailoring, style, and the craft we practice.

How often does Majestic Measure publish new articles?

We publish new editorial content twice monthly exploring topics ranging from fabric science and construction technique to broader questions of personal style, heritage, and culture.

Do your articles cover care and maintenance of tailored garments?

Yes garment care is a recurring theme across our journal. From seasonal storage rituals to pressing techniques and spot-cleaning guidance, we share the knowledge our clients need to preserve their investments.

Can I suggest a topic for The Journal?

Absolutely. We welcome editorial suggestions from our clients and readers. Simply reach out through our Contact page, and our editorial team will review your proposal with genuine interest.

Are the style guides relevant for all body types?

Every article we write is crafted with the full spectrum of body proportions in mind. Bespoke is inherently inclusive and so is our editorial perspective. Whether you are tall, broad, slight, or anywhere in between, the principles of elegant dressing apply universally.

Where can I subscribe to The Journal newsletter?

You can subscribe to our bi-monthly newsletter at the bottom of any page on our website. Subscribers receive early access to new articles, exclusive content, and invitations to private atelier events in London.

Do your authors have formal tailoring training?

Our articles are written by our own atelier team master tailors, cutters, and style consultants who have trained across Savile Row, Naples, and Milan. The knowledge you read is born of real experience, not theory.
