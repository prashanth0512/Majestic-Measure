BESPOKE SUIT ANATOMY

Floating Canvas

A natural horsehair canvas hand-stitched inside the jacket, molding to your body over time for a perfect drape.

The Lapel Roll

A hand-padded, living lapel roll that rolls gracefully to the button point, avoiding the flat crease of factory-made suits.

Soft Shoulder

A natural shoulder line with minimal padding, allowing fluid movement and a clean, elegant silhouette.

Surgeon's Cuffs

Fully functional sleeve buttonholes hand-sewn with silk thread — the hallmark of authentic bespoke heritage.
