THE JOURNEY

Your Tailoring Process

Every bespoke garment follows a careful, unhurried process because excellence cannot be rushed.

1

Consultation

A private session to discuss your lifestyle, aesthetic, and the occasion for your garment. We listen deeply before we begin.

2

Fabric Selection

Browse our curated library of over 4,000 fabrics from the finest mills in Italy, England, and Scotland.

3

Measurements

Over 40 precise measurements are taken, recording not just dimensions but posture, movement, and proportion.

4

Pattern & Cutting

Your unique pattern is drafted and the cloth is hand-cut with surgical precision.

5

Hand Construction

60-80 hours of skilled tailoring. Every stitch contributes to the garment's soul.

6

Fitting & Delivery

Multiple fitting appointments refine the garment until it feels like a second skin.
